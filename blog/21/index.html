5 Gallon Blow Molding Machine

Published 2026-10-24 · KIWL Machinery

The purpose of this guide is to shorten the 5 gallon blow molding machine decision for a blow molding shops buyer. Rate first, then the specification points, then the acceptance checks that turn a quotation into a working machine.

What This Equipment Does

Capacity in bottles per hour is only one figure. The 5 gallon blow molding machine also has to hold 1.5 % weight tolerance, run 2 L format and cycle within the 20-minute changeover window.

Key Technical Specifications

Capacity without conditions is not a specification. 1365–2015 units per hour means little without 2 L product, a 1.5 ml tolerance and a 20-minute changeover — all three stated for this 5 gallon blow molding machine.

Integration With Your Line

A 5 gallon blow molding machine that runs beside the line rather than inside it will always be the bottleneck. This build accepts a standard encoder interface and a 3200 mm working width, which is what lets the blow molding shops production line treat it as one controlled step.

Capacity Planning and ROI

The strongest ROI argument for a 5 gallon blow molding machine is downtime avoided during a 20-minute format change on a 2 L format, because the blow molding shops production line loses output that cannot be recovered later in the day.

Maintenance and Service

The wear items on this 5 gallon blow molding machine are the mould seals, the die lip and the chain drive. None of them should fail without warning, and none should require a week of lead time.

Frequently Asked Questions

What spare parts should be held for the 5 gallon blow molding machine?

The wear items are seals, sensors and drives. For a line running 1365 units per hour, holding these on site protects availability.

Does the 5 gallon blow molding machine need a special foundation?

No special foundation beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 3200 × 3600 × 2100 mm GA drawing.

What accuracy can this 5 gallon blow molding machine hold in real production?

The reference build holds 1.5 ml on 2 L product at 1365–2015 units per hour. Confirm the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
