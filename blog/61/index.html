Double Die Head Blow Molder

Published 2026-11-24 · KIWL Machinery

The double die head blow molder is easier to specify than most blow molding shops buyers expect, because the hard questions have already been asked by other plants. This guide sets out those questions and the answers, in the form of figures you can hold a supplier to.

What This Equipment Does

A double die head blow molder turns molten polymer into a hollow container, and the three things it must control are the parison, the mould and the cooling. This blow molding shops build produces 14570–18570 bottles per hour for 2 L format.

Key Technical Specifications

Ask what a supplier is not willing to write down. The answer, on a double die head blow molder, is usually the tolerance at the low end of the range: this build holds 1.5 ml at 14570 units per hour, not only at 18570.

Integration With Your Line

A double die head blow molder that shares its control protocol with the rest of the blow molding shops production line can be called up by a recipe change. That is what the standard encoder interface on this build is for.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Payback comes from three places: fewer manual steps, less give-away, less downtime. A double die head blow molder handling 14570–18570 units per hour with a 1.5 ml tolerance attacks all three for a blow molding shops operation.

Maintenance and Service

Treat the double die head blow molder service records as production data. Once a few months of interventions are logged, the failure pattern on the seals and the drive usually tells you where the real risk sits.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a format changeover take on this double die head blow molder?

With standardised tooling the changeover is around 15 minutes. The first change to a new 2 L format takes longer.

Can the double die head blow molder run without a dedicated operator?

Once the recipes are stored on the 7-inch colour touch, yes. The blow molding shops team still owns the routine checks, but the 15-minute changeover is guided.

Can the double die head blow molder handle a 2 L product change without full cleaning?

Changeover between compatible formats is a 15-minute operation. A material change needs the cleaning cycle, and the sequence is documented on the 7-inch colour touch.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
