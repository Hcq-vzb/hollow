Parison Control Unit

Published 2026-10-22 · KIWL Machinery

A parison control unit for blow molding shops is a long-term commitment, and the specification is where that commitment is either made explicit or left ambiguous. This guide makes it explicit.

What This Equipment Does

Consider what a parison control unit removes from the blow molding shops process: a step, a wait, or a dependency on operator skill. This configuration handles 8 oz product at 3244–4744 units per hour and is built around that single job.

Key Technical Specifications

Capacity without conditions is not a specification. 3244–4744 units per hour means little without 8 oz product, a 1.5 ml tolerance and a 10-minute changeover — all three stated for this parison control unit.

Integration With Your Line

Integration for a parison control unit means the upstream and downstream stages do not need to change. This build handles 8 oz format at 3244–4744 units per hour and takes the standard signals.

Capacity Planning and ROI

The business case for a parison control unit rests on units that reach the pallet. At 3244–4744 units per hour for 8 oz format, the investment recovers within 12–36 months at regional blow molding shops scale.

Maintenance and Service

The parison control unit was laid out so that the filters, the seals and the sensor positions can be reached without dismantling the frame, which is what makes a routine service possible during a scheduled break.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the parison control unit need a special foundation?

No special foundation beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 1600 × 2200 × 3200 mm GA drawing.

Can the parison control unit be upgraded later?

Within the 3244–4744 band the drive and control have headroom. Going beyond it means a different configuration, so plan the 8 oz range before ordering.

How is support handled after commissioning?

Remote support plus a documented spare parts list. Ask for the response time in writing before ordering.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
