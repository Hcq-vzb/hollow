Benchtop PET Blow Molder

Published 2026-10-24 · KIWL Machinery

Three questions decide most benchtop pet blow molder purchases, and they are the same for every blow molding shops operation: what rate, what accuracy, and what happens when it stops. This guide takes them in that order.

What This Equipment Does

A benchtop pet blow molder occupies one defined station, and the value of that station is decided by how little attention it needs. This blow molding shops build runs at 18922–23422 units per hour on 8 oz product and is specified so a shift operator can run it without specialist knowledge.

Key Technical Specifications

A benchtop pet blow molder is properly specified when an auditor can check it. Rated capacity 18922–23422 units per hour, contact parts 304 stainless steel, control 10-inch colour touch, load 9.5–12.5 kW, footprint 1600 × 1900 × 3200 mm, changeover 8 minutes — this build carries all six.

Integration With Your Line

A benchtop pet blow molder becomes part of the blow molding shops production line when the same recipe reaches it, the same cleaning reaches it and the same fault code reaches the operator. This build is specified around those three handovers.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Model the benchtop pet blow molder on a difficult month rather than an average one. A line running 8 oz product with a 8-minute changeover either holds up or it does not, and the investment should be judged on the harder case.

Maintenance and Service

Insist on a spare parts list with lead times when the benchtop pet blow molder is quoted. A 304 stainless steel contact-part specification without a parts commitment is only half a specification.

Frequently Asked Questions

What spare parts should be held for the benchtop pet blow molder?

The wear items are seals, sensors and drives. For a line running 18922 units per hour, holding these on site protects availability.

Is a 304 stainless steel contact part really necessary?

It determines the cleaning chemistry and the wear life. For corrosive or high-viscosity 8 oz product the standard grade is not appropriate.

What is the actual connected load of the benchtop pet blow molder?

The reference build draws 9.5–12.5 kW. Confirm starting current and supply arrangement at the site.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
