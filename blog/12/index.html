PET Stretch Blow Molder

Published 2026-10-22 · KIWL Machinery

Before any PET stretch blow molder quotation is compared, the rate has to be tied to a product and a format. This guide does that for blow molding shops operations and then works through the rest of the decision.

What This Equipment Does

A PET stretch blow molder occupies one defined station in the production line for blow molding shops. This build runs at 6040–9040 units per hour for 8 oz product, and the configuration at that rate determines output consistency, labour and finished quality.

Key Technical Specifications

Rated capacity of 6040–9040 units per hour is only meaningful alongside the SUS304 with sanitary fittings contact-part grade and the control system, because the cheaper version of each trades directly against accuracy.

Integration With Your Line

Integration for a PET stretch blow molder means the resin handling, the air supply and the downstream take-away agree on one rhythm. This build runs 8 oz format at 6040–9040 bottles per hour and takes the standard signals.

Capacity Planning and ROI

The strongest case for a PET stretch blow molder is the downtime it prevents during a 10-minute changeover. At 6040 units per hour, the blow molding shops production line recovers exactly what the stop takes away.

Maintenance and Service

Availability on a PET stretch blow molder is decided by how quickly a worn part is replaced. Keep the seals, drive and guide rails in stock, because on a 6040–9040 units per hour machine an hour of downtime is a costly hour.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the PET stretch blow molder need a special foundation?

No special foundation beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 2400 × 1400 × 3200 mm GA drawing.

What does the PET stretch blow molder do about product waste at start-up?

The metering system holds the setpoint from the first measurable dose rather than ramping from empty, which is where most give-away occurs.

How long does a format changeover take on this PET stretch blow molder?

With standardised tooling the changeover is around 10 minutes. The first change to a new 8 oz format takes longer.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
