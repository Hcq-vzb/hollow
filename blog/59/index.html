Before any blow molding scrap granulator quotation is compared, the rate has to be tied to a product and a format. This guide does that for blow molding shops operations and then works through the rest of the decision.

What This Equipment Does

A blow molding scrap granulator earns its place by holding its rate through a full campaign. This configuration is specified at 2594–3594 units per hour for 12 oz product, with the tolerance defined rather than implied.

Key Technical Specifications

Where two blow molding scrap granulator quotations agree on output they differ on the parison programming and the cooling, and those are what decide bottle weight on the blow molding shops production line.

Integration With Your Line

Agree the interface list before the order: encoder, safety relay, product detection and CIP return. This blow molding scrap granulator already handles a standard encoder interface and a 1800 mm working width.

Capacity Planning and ROI

The payback case for a blow molding scrap granulator is arithmetic: every unit of 0.3 ml deviation that disappears is material recovered, and every minute of 12-minute changeover that disappears is production time returned to the blow molding shops production line.

Maintenance and Service

The blow molding scrap granulator keeps its wear items accessible and its parameters exportable, so a shift leader can restore a setting without calling a technician and without losing 2594 units per hour of output.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a SUS304 with sanitary fittings contact part really necessary?

It determines the cleaning chemistry and the wear life. For corrosive or high-viscosity 12 oz product the standard grade is not appropriate.

Does the blow molding scrap granulator need a special foundation?

No special foundation beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 1800 × 2800 × 2800 mm GA drawing.

What does the blow molding scrap granulator do about product waste at start-up?

The metering system holds the setpoint from the first measurable dose rather than ramping from empty, which is where most give-away occurs.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
