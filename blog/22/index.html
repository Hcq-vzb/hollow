PP Bottle Blow Molding Machine

Published 2026-10-24 · KIWL Machinery

This guide is written for the person who signs the order and the person who will run the machine. Both need the same blow molding shops figures, presented so that neither has to translate them first.

What This Equipment Does

A pp bottle blow molding machine occupies one defined station in the production line for blow molding shops. This build runs at 4476–5976 units per hour for 5 L product, and the configuration at that rate determines output consistency, labour and finished quality.

Key Technical Specifications

Do not compare a pp bottle blow molding machine on rated capacity alone. A 4476–5976 units per hour machine with a 1.5 ml tolerance and 304 stainless steel contact parts will out-produce a faster machine with looser accuracy over a full shift.

Integration With Your Line

Agree the interface list for the pp bottle blow molding machine with the shift team: resin feed, air, take-away and the signal that says a bottle is good enough to leave the machine.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Model the pp bottle blow molding machine on total cost over the equipment life, not on purchase price. A unit with 304 stainless steel contact parts and a 1.5 ml tolerance costs more to buy and less to run than a cheaper unit on the same blow molding shops production line.

Maintenance and Service

The wear items on this pp bottle blow molding machine are the mould seals, the die lip and the chain drive. None of them should fail without warning, and none should require a week of lead time.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the actual connected load of the pp bottle blow molding machine?

The reference build draws 3–4 kW. Confirm starting current and supply arrangement at the site.

How is support handled after commissioning?

Remote support plus a documented spare parts list. Ask for the response time in writing before ordering.

Is a 304 stainless steel contact part really necessary?

It determines the cleaning chemistry and the wear life. For corrosive or high-viscosity 5 L product the standard grade is not appropriate.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
