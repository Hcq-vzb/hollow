What This Equipment Does

A continuous extrusion blow molder occupies one defined station, and the value of that station is decided by how little attention it needs. This blow molding shops build runs at 2379–3379 units per hour on 5 L product and is specified so a shift operator can run it without specialist knowledge.

Key Technical Specifications

For a blow molding shops buyer the continuous extrusion blow molder specification worth writing down is: 2379–3379 bottles per hour, 5 L format, 0.5 % weight tolerance, 3 layers, 7-inch colour touch controls and SUS304 with sanitary fittings contact parts.

Integration With Your Line

A continuous extrusion blow molder that stores its parison programme on the 7-inch colour touch makes the blow molding shops production line flexible. This build changes format in 20 minutes with the programme reloaded automatically.

The business case for a continuous extrusion blow molder rests on bottles that reach the pallet at the right weight. At 2379–3379 bottles per hour for 5 L format, the investment recovers within 12–36 months at regional blow molding shops scale.

Maintenance and Service

Frequently Asked Questions

What spare parts should be held for the continuous extrusion blow molder?

The wear items are seals, sensors and drives. For a line running 2379 units per hour, holding these on site protects availability.

What accuracy can this continuous extrusion blow molder hold in real production?

The reference build holds 0.5 ml on 5 L product at 2379–3379 units per hour. Confirm the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.

What does the continuous extrusion blow molder do about product waste at start-up?

The metering system holds the setpoint from the first measurable dose rather than ramping from empty, which is where most give-away occurs.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?
