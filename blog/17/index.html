Mold Temperature Controller

Published 2026-10-22 · KIWL Machinery

This guide is written for the person who signs the order and the person who will run the machine. Both need the same blow molding shops figures, presented so that neither has to translate them first.

What This Equipment Does

Consider what a mold temperature controller removes from the blow molding shops process: a step, a wait, or a dependency on operator skill. This configuration handles 1.5 L product at 6013–9013 units per hour and is built around that single job.

Key Technical Specifications

The reference specification for this mold temperature controller is 6013–9013 units per hour, 1.5 L format, 304/316L stainless steel parts, a 10-inch colour touch control and a 1800 × 2800 × 2400 mm footprint.

Integration With Your Line

Downstream transfer matters as much as upstream buffer. If the mold temperature controller can hand over at 6013–9013 units per hour without accumulation, the rest of the line keeps its own cadence.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Payback comes from three places: fewer manual steps, less give-away, less downtime. A mold temperature controller handling 6013–9013 units per hour with a 0.5 ml tolerance attacks all three for a blow molding shops operation.

Maintenance and Service

For a blow molding shops operation the maintenance question for a mold temperature controller is simple: can the wear parts be changed by your own team during a scheduled break rather than by a technician on site?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the actual connected load of the mold temperature controller?

The reference build draws 15–22 kW. Confirm starting current and supply arrangement at the site.

Can the mold temperature controller handle a 1.5 L product change without full cleaning?

Changeover between compatible formats is a 15-minute operation. A material change needs the cleaning cycle, and the sequence is documented on the 10-inch colour touch.

What accuracy can this mold temperature controller hold in real production?

The reference build holds 0.5 ml on 1.5 L product at 6013–9013 units per hour. Confirm the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
