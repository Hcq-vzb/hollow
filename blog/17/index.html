Mold Temperature Controller

Published 2026-10-22 · KIWL Machinery

Choosing the right mold temperature controller is a practical decision for blow molding shops. This guide covers the criteria that matter when specifying, integrating and accepting a mold temperature controller on a production line, with the figures a buyer should confirm in writing before placing an order.

What This Equipment Does

A mold temperature controller occupies one defined station, and the value of that station is decided by how little attention it needs. This blow molding shops build runs at 6013–9013 units per hour on 1.5 L product and is specified so a shift operator can run it without specialist knowledge.

Key Technical Specifications

For a blow molding shops buyer, the specification worth writing into the order is: 304/316L stainless steel product-contact parts, 10-inch colour touch controls, 0.5 ml tolerance, 15-minute changeover, 15–22 kW connected load and a 1800 × 2800 × 2400 mm footprint.

Integration With Your Line

The best commissioning plan names three people: one for the 0.5 ml verification, one for the encoder handshake, one for the cleaning route. With a 6013–9013 units per hour machine, that is what a week-long delay costs.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Size the mold temperature controller against peak demand with modest headroom, then model payback from labour, yield and downtime rather than from price. At 6013–9013 units per hour a typical blow molding shops operation recovers the investment within 12–36 months at regional scale.

Maintenance and Service

Record every intervention against the mold temperature controller. After a few months the pattern of the seals and the drive tells you the real service interval, which is usually shorter than the manual claims.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the rated capacity of this unit?

The reference build handles 6013–9013 units per hour depending on the configuration.

What maintenance does it need?

Routine checks on the wear items and a scheduled service at the recommended interval.

Can it be integrated with the line PLC?

Yes, standard signal interfaces allow it to run under the line PLC.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
