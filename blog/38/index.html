Frequently Asked Questions

What accuracy can this blow molding machine for wide mouth jars hold in real production?

The reference build holds 1.5 ml on 60–120 mm diameter product at 18814–23314 units per hour. Confirm the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.

Is a food-grade 304 stainless steel contact part really necessary?

It determines the cleaning chemistry and the wear life. For corrosive or high-viscosity 60–120 mm diameter product the standard grade is not appropriate.

Is the blow molding machine for wide mouth jars suitable for continuous three-shift running?

The reference build is rated for continuous duty in the 18814–23314 band. For heavier duty ask for the upgraded drive package.

How do I size this for my plant?
