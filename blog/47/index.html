What This Equipment Does

Capacity in bottles per hour is only one figure. The blow molding machine maintenance guide also has to hold 2.0 % weight tolerance, run 1.5 L format and cycle within the 15-minute changeover window.

Key Technical Specifications

A blow molding machine maintenance guide is properly specified when an auditor can check it. Integration for a blow molding machine maintenance guide means the resin handling, the air supply and the downstream take-away agree on one rhythm. This build runs 1.5 L format at 14810–18810 bottles per hour and takes the standard signals.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Include the service contract in the ROI. A blow molding machine maintenance guide with a 7-inch colour touch and a 15-minute changeover loses far less output when a fault occurs, because the diagnosis is quicker and the reset is shorter.

Maintenance and Service

Put the blow molding machine maintenance guide on a cycle-based schedule. At 14810–18810 bottles per hour the mould seals reach end of life long before a monthly inspection notices.
