The 20 liter bottle blow molding machine is easier to specify than most blow molding shops buyers expect, because the hard questions have already been asked by other plants. This guide sets out those questions and the answers, in the form of figures you can hold a supplier to.

What This Equipment Does

Every machine on a line should have one sentence that explains why it exists. For this 20 liter bottle blow molding machine it is: it processes 330 ml product at 3414–4914 units per hour, consistently, without the blow molding shops team chasing it around.

Key Technical Specifications

Do not compare a 20 liter bottle blow molding machine on rated capacity alone. A 3414–4914 units per hour machine with a 2.0 ml tolerance and 316L stainless steel contact parts will out-produce a faster machine with looser accuracy over a full shift.

Integration With Your Line

A blow moulding line is only as good as the handshake between the extruder and the mould. This 20 liter bottle blow molding machine accepts the standard encoder interface across a 1800 mm working width, so the parison and the mould stay in phase.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Look at the 20 liter bottle blow molding machine over five years rather than one budget cycle. 316L stainless steel contact parts, a 2.0 ml tolerance and a 10-minute changeover have different cost curves, and the purchase price tells you very little about any of them.

Maintenance and Service

Record every intervention against the 20 liter bottle blow molding machine. After a few months the pattern of the mould seal tells a blow molding shops operation the real service interval.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 20 liter bottle blow molding machine behave if the upstream stops?

The buffer upstream of the 20 liter bottle blow molding machine determines the recovery. The machine itself restarts into the stored recipe once the upstream signal returns.

Does the 20 liter bottle blow molding machine need a special foundation?

No special foundation beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 1800 × 3600 × 2800 mm GA drawing.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.
