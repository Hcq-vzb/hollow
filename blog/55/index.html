Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a format changeover take on this 55 gallon drum blow molding machine?

With standardised tooling the changeover is around 12 minutes. The first change to a new 16 oz format takes longer.

What does the 55 gallon drum blow molding machine do about product waste at start-up?

The metering system holds the setpoint from the first measurable dose rather than ramping from empty, which is where most give-away occurs.

Does the 55 gallon drum blow molding machine need a special foundation?
