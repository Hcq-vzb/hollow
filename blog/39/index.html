Blow Pin And Blow Needle

Published 2026-11-03 · KIWL Machinery

The blow pin and blow needle is easier to specify than most blow molding shops buyers expect, because the hard questions have already been asked by other plants. This guide sets out those questions and the answers, in the form of figures you can hold a supplier to.

What This Equipment Does

The blow pin and blow needle is where raw material becomes a finished unit, and that conversion is where a blow molding shops operation's cost is set. At 3959–5459 units per hour for 12 oz product, this build makes that conversion repeatable.

Key Technical Specifications

Do not compare a blow pin and blow needle on rated capacity alone. A 3959–5459 units per hour machine with a 1.0 ml tolerance and 304/316L stainless steel contact parts will out-produce a faster machine with looser accuracy over a full shift.

Integration With Your Line

Before ordering a blow pin and blow needle, write the interface list and assign an owner to each item: encoder, safety relay, product detection, cleaning return. This configuration already handles a standard encoder interface and a 1800 mm working width.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Compare the blow pin and blow needle against the cost of the manual step it removes, including the labour during changeover. At 12 minutes per format change and 3959 units per hour, the saving is measurable in shift hours.

Maintenance and Service

A blow pin and blow needle that is easy to maintain is one your team will actually maintain. On this build the wear items are visible, the intervals are published and the reset procedure fits on one page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the blow pin and blow needle handle a 12 oz product change without full cleaning?

Changeover between compatible formats is a 12-minute operation. A material change needs the cleaning cycle, and the sequence is documented on the 4-inch HMI.

Can the blow pin and blow needle run without a dedicated operator?

Once the recipes are stored on the 4-inch HMI, yes. The blow molding shops team still owns the routine checks, but the 12-minute changeover is guided.

How long does a format changeover take on this blow pin and blow needle?

With standardised tooling the changeover is around 12 minutes. The first change to a new 12 oz format takes longer.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
