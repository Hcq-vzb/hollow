Semi-Automatic Blow Molding Machine

Published 2026-10-24 · KIWL Machinery

The purpose of this guide is to shorten the semi-automatic blow molding machine decision for a blow molding shops buyer. Rate first, then the specification points, then the acceptance checks that turn a quotation into a working machine.

What This Equipment Does

A semi-automatic blow molding machine earns its place by holding its rate through a full campaign. This configuration is specified at 1650–2300 units per hour for 16 oz product, with the tolerance defined rather than implied.

Key Technical Specifications

This semi-automatic blow molding machine draws 1.5–2.5 kW and occupies 1200 × 1400 × 2100 mm, uses food-grade 304 stainless steel contact parts and holds a 1.0 ml tolerance on 16 oz product.

Integration With Your Line

A semi-automatic blow molding machine that stores its parison programme on the 10-inch colour touch makes the blow molding shops production line flexible. This build changes format in 10 minutes with the programme reloaded automatically.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Model the semi-automatic blow molding machine on a difficult month rather than an average one. A line running 16 oz product with a 10-minute changeover either holds up or it does not, and the investment should be judged on the harder case.

Maintenance and Service

Availability on a semi-automatic blow molding machine is decided by how quickly a worn part is replaced. Keep the seals, drive and guide rails in stock, because on a 1650–2300 units per hour machine an hour of downtime is a costly hour.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are settings recorded for traceability?

The 10-inch colour touch stores a recipe per format and the parameters can be exported, which matters for blow molding shops operations running audits.

What does the semi-automatic blow molding machine do about product waste at start-up?

The metering system holds the setpoint from the first measurable dose rather than ramping from empty, which is where most give-away occurs.

What accuracy can this semi-automatic blow molding machine hold in real production?

The reference build holds 1.0 ml on 16 oz product at 1650–2300 units per hour. Confirm the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
