Parison Programmer System

Published 2026-11-27 · KIWL Machinery

A parison programmer system for blow molding shops is judged on the specification and then lived with for years. This guide covers what to ask for, what to verify on the floor, and what to keep in stock afterwards.

What This Equipment Does

A parison programmer system earns a place on the blow molding shops production line by removing variation rather than by adding capacity. This build holds 1629–2279 units per hour on 2 L product, which is what makes downstream planning possible.

Key Technical Specifications

Ask the supplier to state the range the rate applies to. A parison programmer system quoting 1629–2279 units per hour on 2 L format has to say whether the tolerance holds across that range.

Integration With Your Line

Run the integration review with the people who operate the line, not only the people who install it. The parison programmer system is mechanically compatible with common conveyors, but the blow molding shops workflow decides how it is actually used.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Look at the parison programmer system over five years rather than one budget cycle. 316L stainless steel contact parts, a 1.5 ml tolerance and a 8-minute changeover have different cost curves, and the purchase price tells you very little about any of them.

Maintenance and Service

Every intervention on the parison programmer system should be recorded against the unit serial and the hour meter. Six months of that data gives a blow molding shops operation its real service intervals for the 316L stainless steel contact parts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the parison programmer system be upgraded later?

Within the 1629–2279 band the drive and control have headroom. Going beyond it means a different configuration, so plan the 2 L range before ordering.

What spare parts should be held for the parison programmer system?

The wear items are seals, sensors and drives. For a line running 1629 units per hour, holding these on site protects availability.

How is support handled after commissioning?

Remote support plus a documented spare parts list. Ask for the response time in writing before ordering.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
