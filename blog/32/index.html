High Speed Blow Molding Machine

Published 2026-10-27 · KIWL Machinery

Blow moulding is a three-stage process — extrude, shape, cool — and a high speed blow molding machine for blow molding shops has to hold all three. This guide covers the output, the container range and the wall control that decide the fit.

What This Equipment Does

A high speed blow molding machine earns a place on the blow molding shops production line by removing variation rather than by adding capacity. This build holds 7413–10413 units per hour on 1.5 L product, which is what makes downstream planning possible.

Key Technical Specifications

The reference specification for this high speed blow molding machine is 7413–10413 bottles per hour, 2–3 L, 2.0 % weight tolerance, 7-inch colour touch control and a 3200 × 1900 × 3200 mm footprint.

Integration With Your Line

Integration work on a high speed blow molding machine is mostly deciding who owns what. Once the encoder, the cleaning route and the recipe store are agreed with the blow molding shops production line, commissioning stops being a negotiation.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Compare the high speed blow molding machine against the cost of the manual step it removes, including the labour during changeover. At 15 minutes per format change and 7413 units per hour, the saving is measurable in shift hours.

Maintenance and Service

Build the maintenance plan from the duty cycle rather than the calendar. At 7413–10413 units per hour the high speed blow molding machine accumulates hours faster than any inspection schedule assumes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a format changeover take on this high speed blow molding machine?

With standardised tooling the changeover is around 15 minutes. The first change to a new 1.5 L format takes longer.

What accuracy can this high speed blow molding machine hold in real production?

The reference build holds 2.0 ml on 1.5 L product at 7413–10413 units per hour. Confirm the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.

How is the high speed blow molding machine documented for the buyer?

The documentation set covers the exploded view, electrical drawings, the parameter backup procedure and the spare parts list with lead times.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
