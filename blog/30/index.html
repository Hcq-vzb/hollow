Blow Molding Mold Making

Published 2026-10-26 · KIWL Machinery

The difference between a workable blow molding mold making and an expensive mistake usually shows up six months later, in downtime and in service calls. This guide covers the specification points that decide the outcome for blow molding shops, in the order they are best resolved.

What This Equipment Does

Capacity in bottles per hour is only one figure. The blow molding mold making also has to hold 3.0 % weight tolerance, run 330 ml can format and cycle within the 15-minute changeover window.

Key Technical Specifications

The three figures that move the price most are accuracy, contact-part grade and the control platform. On this blow molding mold making the tolerance is 3.0 ml, the contact parts are 316L stainless steel, and the controls run on a 7-inch colour touch.

Integration With Your Line

A blow molding mold making that shares its control protocol with the rest of the blow molding shops production line can be called up by a recipe change. That is what the standard encoder interface on this build is for.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Model the blow molding mold making on a difficult month rather than an average one. A line running 330 ml can product with a 15-minute changeover either holds up or it does not, and the investment should be judged on the harder case.

Maintenance and Service

Insist on a spare parts list with lead times when the blow molding mold making is quoted. A 316L stainless steel contact-part specification without a parts commitment is only half a specification.
